BUILT ON EMERGENCE

Familiar workflows. Open tools.

Our direction takes cues from SolidWorks’ feature-based mechanical workflow, Altium’s connected PCB workflow and Cadence’s silicon implementation tools. These independent prototypes are early steps toward that depth.

Use today

Mechanical: dimensioned rectangular or circular profiles, extrusion, standard views, revisioned assemblies, Rust checks and shop drawings. PCB: footprint placement, net assignments, two-layer pad connections, geometry checks and BOM. Silicon: editable block hierarchy, core constraints, placement checks and floorplan SVG.

Next engineering milestones

General sketch constraints, B-rep/STEP and GD&T; schematic capture, obstacle-aware routing and PCB fabrication packages; PDK-backed IC layout, synthesis integration, timing, DRC/LVS and GDSII. PCB and silicon files are currently separate from the mechanical revision graph and peer sync. Each editor states which checks it runs.

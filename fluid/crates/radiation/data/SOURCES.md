Numeric optical data are distributed under the refractiveindex.info database
CC0 dedication. No article prose is reproduced. Wavelengths are micrometres;
`n` and `k` are dimensionless. Full original tables are retained.

- Water at 25 °C: G. M. Hale and M. R. Querry (1973),
  https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.12.000555.
  https://github.com/polyanskiy/refractiveindex.info-database/blob/master/database/data/main/H2O/nk/Hale.yml
- Iron at room temperature: P. B. Johnson and R. W. Christy (1974),
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.9.5056.
  https://github.com/polyanskiy/refractiveindex.info-database/blob/master/database/data/main/Fe/nk/Johnson.yml

Tables fetched 2026-10-05. The checked-in CSV contents, rather than a live
upstream branch, are the reproducible inputs. Linear interpolation of n,k
is used only inside the measured range in the demo. Room-temperature iron
data do not certify the optics of molten iron.

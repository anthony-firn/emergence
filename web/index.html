Read the error with the picture. Viscosity is integrated exactly inside fourth-order Runge–Kutta; the nonlinear term is dealiased with the 2/3 rule. The outer-shell energy fraction is a resolution warning, not an error bound. These small grids are for exploration. Re = 1600 turbulence needs much finer native runs for accurate dissipation.
